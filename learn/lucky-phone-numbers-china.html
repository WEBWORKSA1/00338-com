A Chengdu number, +86 28 8888 8888, sold for CN¥2.33 million in 2003, and a Beijing number ending 88888 fetched CN¥2.25 million at a 2020 court auction. The last four digits carry most of the value.

Checklist for choosing a number

  1. Focus on the last 4 digits. That is what people remember and read.
  2. Avoid 4, 14, 24, 74 and 250.
  3. Prefer 8, 6, 9 and patterns: AAAA, AABB, ABAB, ABCD rising.
  4. Match your audience's dialect. Cantonese customers love 28; Mandarin ones love 168 and 518.
  5. Match your brand. A florist may want 520; a finance firm 888 or 1688.

Frequently asked questions

What is a 靓号?

A "beautiful number" — a phone number with auspicious digits (8, 6, 9) or patterns (AAAA, ABAB, 168, 518).

Which phone endings are luckiest?

8888, 6666, 9999, 168, 518, 1688 and AABB patterns like 6688.

Should I avoid 4 in my business number?

For Chinese-speaking customers, yes — especially at the end.
