Lucky Red Packet (Hongbao) Amounts: How Much to Give and Numbers to Avoid

By the 00338 Number Lab editorial team · Updated 2026-09-28 · 5 min read
Quick answer: Give even amounts with lucky digits — 88, 168, 288, 666, 888 — and avoid anything with 4 or the amount 250. Odd amounts are traditionally associated with funerals. Use new, crisp notes.

Red packets (红包 hóngbāo / 利是 lai6 si6 in Cantonese) are given at Lunar New Year, weddings, birthdays and business openings. The amount itself is a message.

Quick reference

OccasionGood amountsWhy
Lunar New Year (kids)20, 50, 88, 100Even, round, 88 = double prosperity
Wedding388*, 688, 888, 1688Prosperity, smoothness
Partner520, 1314I love you / forever
Business opening168, 888, 1688Prosperity all the way
Staff bonus66, 88, 168Smooth progress

* 388 reads "生发发" in Cantonese; in Mandarin contexts some prefer 688 because of the 38 slang.

Never give

  • Anything with 4 (40, 400, 444)
  • 250 — "idiot"
  • Odd amounts at happy occasions
  • Coins or old, torn notes

Frequently asked questions

How much should I give at a Chinese wedding?

Typically enough to cover your meal at the banquet, rounded to a lucky even amount like 688, 888 or 1,688 in local currency. Local norms vary.

Is 520 a good red-packet amount?

Yes, between partners — it means "I love you". Many digital red packets are capped at 200, so people send 5.20 or 52.0 online.

What amounts are for funerals?

Odd amounts are traditionally used in white envelopes (帛金) for funerals.
