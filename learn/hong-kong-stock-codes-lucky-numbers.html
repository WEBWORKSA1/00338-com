Lucky Stock Codes: How Number Symbolism Shows Up in Hong Kong Tickers

By the 00338 Number Lab editorial team · Updated 2026-09-28 · 6 min read
Quick answer: Hong Kong securities use five-digit codes (e.g. 00338, 00005). Codes are assigned by the exchange, but investors and issuers in Chinese markets notice lucky digits (8, 6, 9) and avoid 4 when they can choose. Symbolism is cultural colour — it is never a reason to buy or sell.

Five-digit codes are Hong Kong's ticker system. Leading zeros are dropped on many data sites, so 00338, 0338.HK and 338.HK all point to the same listing.

Why codes get noticed

  • Codes are easy to remember and read as numbers, so investors notice 8s and 4s just like on plates or phones.
  • Low codes (single and double digits) belong to many of the oldest listings — a mark of heritage.
  • Where issuers have a choice, auspicious digits are sometimes preferred.

Frequently asked questions

How are Hong Kong stock codes formatted?

Five digits, often written with leading zeros (00338) or shortened (0338.HK / 338.HK) on data sites.

Do lucky stock codes outperform?

There is no reliable evidence that they do. Treat code symbolism as culture, not a signal.

Where can I look up a Hong Kong code?

The exchange's official disclosure site, HKEXnews.
